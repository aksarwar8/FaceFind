Local processing and model rights

Inference runs on this device without external recognition APIs. SCRFD pretrained weights are restricted to non-commercial research. AdaFace checkpoint rights remain unresolved. SCRFD + AdaFace is the research demo configuration. YuNet + SFace is the bundled fallback.
